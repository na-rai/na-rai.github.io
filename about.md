# About

## Background

I'm Nazifa Anjum, a Computer Science and Engineering student currently in my 4th semester. I'm interested in computer science because of how broad the field is. There are so many different areas to explore, and I enjoy the fact that CSE gives me the freedom to move between building software, solving problems, working with algorithms, exploring intelligent systems, and understanding how things work underneath the surface.

I'm particularly drawn to the process of building things. I like taking an idea and turning it into something that actually works, even when getting there involves a considerable amount of debugging, questionable decisions, and staring at an error message for far too long. My coursework has given me experience with programming, data structures and algorithms, databases, computer systems, electronics, networking, and different areas of software and intelligent systems.

I'm still exploring where I want to specialize, and I don't see that as something I need to decide immediately. One of the things I value most about CSE is having the opportunity to keep discovering new areas and figuring out what I genuinely enjoy.

## What I care about

I care about learning how things work rather than simply learning how to use them. I'm interested in problem-solving, algorithms, software development, and the process of turning abstract ideas into working systems.

I'm also interested in the creative side of technology. Building something from scratch, experimenting with an idea, and seeing it gradually become functional is one of the parts of CSE I find most rewarding.

Outside of academics, I enjoy reading books and exploring different subjects simply because they catch my curiosity. I also enjoy music and tend to have a fairly eclectic taste. I like having interests outside of computer science because they give me space to think differently and keep me curious beyond coursework.
